**How it works:** seat allocation is a single atomic `incr` on a per-event counter — each caller gets a distinct count, and whoever pushes it past 10 rolls their own increment back, so it's race-safe without locking at the caller level. `get`/`set` record each customer's seat so a repeat call returns the same seat instead of double-booking; `expire` is used to model an optional timed reservation window (e.g., a flash sale) that resets the whole event's seats once it lapses.
